## P58 source-to-experiment precommit
Read [Shin 2017](https://doi.org/10.5334/gjgl.143) to separate referential true-partitives from kind-denoting pseudo-partitive/quantitative structures, *not* to claim this paper gold-labels the word `그중`. [Song 2021](https://doi.org/10.17002/sil..60.202107.89) analyzes an **adjacent internally headed relative-clause** subtype with 'among' substitution; does not license converting that diagnostic into direct partitive gold. The already-owned [Ham 2026](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1q_1MyyChyI4_h1ErQsNS1g4ttHiZuzaK/view), [Roh et al. 2005](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1NU0FFg_k0HGJqaCiHlAJpWS81XuL-JaC/view), and [Choe 2021](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cKp51GEWKfzr99WDHUA7geX4fkbVNWBd/view) PDFs were **actually read** this turn: Korean information structure should be context-first, zero reference depends on discourse center transitions, and null/overt pronoun choices cannot be reduced to bare textual proximity. The five new English-language style-evaluation PDFs are relevant method donors, **not Korean referential gold**.
