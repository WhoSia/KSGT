# R9.41 — Korean subject choice with appearance, competition, and resolution

Four authored story worlds each retain the intended actor and next action across three conditions: one plausible actor, a second actor performing a similar act, and the same additional actor explicitly leaving. These contexts are new synthetic items. They operationalize a reading candidate inventory; they are not prospective reader annotations. A controlled realizer receives hand-authored focus updates and renders the selected next sentence. The baseline model receives the full Korean prefix and the intended actor and action, free wording in one sentence. The baseline's access to actual language and known intended actor differs from the realizer's access to author-provided focus updates.

Count named intended subjects, wrong identities, bare or indirect forms, action preservation, and lexical omissions separately. In the competing condition the request explicitly calls for a name. In one-actor and resolved cases omission is permitted, and an overt name is also allowed. A name in the latter conditions is only an opportunity to omit under the operational plan. Human readability remains unmeasured; departure alone does not guarantee a particular reading. Record any naturalization, paraphrase or context change that could influence interpretation.
