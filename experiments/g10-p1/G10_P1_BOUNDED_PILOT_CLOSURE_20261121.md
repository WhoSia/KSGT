# KSGT Generation X — G10-P1 Bounded Pilot Closure & P2 Transition (2026-11-21)

**Official disposition:** G10-P1 CLOSED as a bounded **research/development pilot**, not as a positively validated writing-quality result. **P2 explicitly authorized** under the selected full formal name. The legacy P1 historical results and proofs remain available, without deleting false starts or errors.

## What closed
- Real provider and cross-model Korean generation, source-work paired strong baselines, actual token/latency receipts, three-paragraph outputs and provenance-preserving archive.
- Audited correction of the R9.1 KoLLA candidate attribution mistake in R9.3; human agreement 432/1,410 is not model output performance.
- Fixed-condition discourse/reader-state implementations; narrow schema/surface/scope tests and independently inspectable failures.
- R9.34's 12 project-authored briefs × 3 real completions and R9.44's separated reader and source-audit forms (four forms on the **same 12 works**, no external reader ratings).
- [Retrospective and P2 gates](paper/R9_44_G10_P1_RETROSPECTIVE_P2_NAMING_20261121.md).

## What demonstrably did NOT close as scientific performance
- No independently validated Korean reader preference, source audit or actual edit-work reduction.
- No source-work-disjoint confirmatory experiment. Exposed development cohorts cannot be renamed heldout.
- No model/compute-matched end-to-end gain of state architecture over a strong direct/rich or same-information prompt comparator.
- No successful Transformer or recurrent/SSM alternative training. State annotation costs and author labor were not end-to-end measured.
- R9.30 grouped-note output gave answer findability 6/6 but no joint discourse-purpose+fidelity success; R9.42 full-sentence output 20/20 pairs unchanged with annotated inventory. A successful internal test is not a reader preference claim.
