These are short, concrete audits, not eight independent observations of model superiority. Their units are individual source works and arms. The original author-screen already classified many outputs as source breaches; this audit adds a mechanism-level distinction and disputes the simplistic treatment of redundant but consistent notice rows.

## The evaluation design must not conflate different tasks

1. **Reader-first stage (no source facts visible)**: ask what the text communicates, whether it can be used, the first phrase to edit, and what the text seems to promise or conclude. Do not present a list of "AI flaws" before the first reaction; doing so primes the participant. Show anonymous arm codes and include ties, both unusable and undecidable.
2. **Source-audit stage (facts now visible)**: give all original fact sentences, not just the vague user request. Require the auditor to distinguish supported fact, permitted inference, new fact/condition/commitment, omission and uncertainty. For source-sensitive tasks, a fluent text can fail this gate. For literature, separate fixed required events from optional texture.
3. **Interpretation and rhetorical stage**: independently ask whether the reader identifies the intended referent and conditional actions, understands a scientific qualification, or reads an over-explained ending. First edit position and reason are a more actionable measure of discomfort than detecting a banned lexical phrase.
4. **Prospective experimental control**: preserve strong direct generation and a strong general writing brief; add only one purpose-based intervention per matched work. Record output length and per-call tokens, avoid constraining literary paragraph counts without an independent reason, and pre-register the effect before seeing outputs. Do not count four existing permutations of the same twelve works as 48 independent samples.
5. **Human outcome gate**: do not replace blind reader data with the investigator's own breach screen, auto metrics or self-designed state gold. Compare full-passage usability and actual edit burden before advancing a new generator or training an alternative architecture.

## Architectural decision

The R9.42 actor-state work is useful chiefly as a warning against overconfident exclusion. It did not improve natural sentences in a matched same-information comparison. R9.34 shows a larger, under-addressed failure: AI writing can invent commitments, stretch causal claims, add redundant structural signposts, or explain away a literary ending even if local reference rules are satisfied. The main KSGT work should therefore return to **complete reader-facing prose** and use finite state operations selectively for source-grounded violations; direct generation remains the practical reference.

The experimental prompt in R9.34 prohibited all new facts in every genre. This is appropriate for a fixed laboratory report or an exact notice, but could penalize harmless literary texture and induce overly restricted fiction. A future writing comparison must declare separate budgets for fixed events, allowed incidental detail, and invented events. Do not reinterpret the existing R9.34 breach ratios as a general measure of fiction quality.
