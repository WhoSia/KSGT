# R9.30 — Decision routes, discriminating observations and narrative focus

G10-P1 OPEN · Development protocol frozen before generation · 2026-11-17 KST

R9.29 found two gains from paragraph plans in practical notices, an explanatory plan that lost the relation between two hypotheses and a measurement, and a direct scene whose ending substantially reproduced the factual brief. This round tests relations inside complete texts. Each of six new authored public briefs uses short source fragments, two per genre.

- Notice plans bind a condition to its action and any exception. The reader must recover a specific route.
- Explanation plans bind a pair of candidate explanations to distinguishable outcomes of one proposed observation. The reader must understand how the outcomes bear on the explanations and recognize the limits of the test.
- Scene plans bind viewpoint and pacing decisions to events in the source. The prose must achieve the work's particular closing effect. For narrative, rate the effect through the movement of attention and syntax, not any fixed lexical choice. The license allows expressive realization but excludes new world facts.

Three one-call arms share model, temperature, completion limit, factual fragments, license, audience and writing goal. Strong direct gets that brief alone. Flat notes gets author annotations as strings in a predeclared interleaved order. Grouped notes gets exactly those same strings organized into two genre-specific relations. This joint intervention tests grouping, labeling and order. It does not identify their separate effects or establish that a system can infer annotations automatically. Use the same GPT-OSS 20B Groq configuration as R9.29. One response per cell, eighteen total.

Before label reveal, read the complete outputs against the frozen checks in the fixture. Record reader-task answerability and the intended effect separately as clear, ambiguous or failed. Count source or license breaches separately, with exact spans or a short identifying phrase. Count blank-line-delimited paragraphs separately. Preserve reader questions and measurable effects even when the output also contains a breach. No human preference claim follows from author-side scoring.

Retain grouped notes for another development round in a given genre only if at least one of its works gains a strict intended-effect pass over flat notes, neither loses a strict pass relative to flat or direct, and neither grouped output contains a material breach. A gain concentrated in any single work does not justify a production default. Keep direct examples and identify any expressive difference that requires a reader judgment, without requiring reader participation for the engineering run.
