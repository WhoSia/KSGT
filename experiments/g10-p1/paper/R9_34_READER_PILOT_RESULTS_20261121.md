The source screening is a single investigator's diagnostic read. Candidate labels were shuffled within a work before scoring. Writing structure can still reveal the condition. The issue column includes changed event, actor, time, inference, and unlicensed obligations or promises. It uses the strict factual and event license established in the prompts. For an explicit 14:00 exception phrased alongside 이전 or 이후, we marked uncertainty rather than calling a definite communication failure. There is no winner estimate for readers or novel genres.

## Examples and next use

1. NOTICE_SETUP (guided) maps booth application to arrival time and electric hookup application to a note. All three can yield the targeted answer. Direct additionally requires notifying a desk and restoring equipment; rich broadens the addressees to teams that need power and instructs attendees to submit the note. Both add obligations. Run the reader question separately from source screening.
2. SCI_CARD (guided) retains equal droplet volumes and cleaned, dried films without inserting a solvent, a volume or a contact-angle measurement. SCI_LENS (guided) still says the existing observations show strong dependence on screen position, although distance and screen both changed. SCI_SCALE (guided) retains the shared-offset premise and then introduces an internally unclear sentence about 0.4 g. The benefit is localized.
3. Every scene produced at least one unlicensed addition across all arms on this small cohort. A prescribed endpoint question can verify who did what; it does not tell us whether a real reader prefers the prose. Preserve both potential reader preference and source fidelity as separate observations.

The packet generator creates four forms. Across forms a work appears as guided–direct and guided–rich in both A/B orientations. Each form shows a work once. Reader files carry prompts and candidates without condition names or scoring keys; detailed facts, keys and investigator source screening live in the restricted evaluation archive. Readers can return both comprehension answers and A/B/tie/neither/undecided, along with the first requested edit. No readers were contacted and no ratings were entered.
