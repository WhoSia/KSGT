# R9.34 — New-work Korean Reader Pilot

Twelve new synthetic tasks, three per genre (chat, science, notice, scene). Each work is the unit. It contributes three one-call candidates under the same GPT-OSS 20B settings and fixed reader, source, request and freedom of length. Direct includes a factual guard. Rich adds common actionable writing guidance. Guided adds one hand-authored, work-specific choice to Rich. The planned contrast tests the added cue, including its extra input tokens, not algorithmic planning or a token-matched mechanism. All six request orders occur twice.

Record independent reader choice for guided vs. rich and guided vs. direct, with ties and insufficient information allowed. Include a task-specific question, the first edit and an optional reason. Separate source-fidelity review with access to the brief from reader judgment. Readers get the task and candidates without condition labels, source facts, or the scoring key. Split which comparison and screen position each reader sees. Across four forms each work has both comparisons in both positions, one pair per work in each form. Reports must preserve identical pairs as meaningful ties and record missing responses and the number of readers and works. The scripted ratings fixture is not human evidence. No reader recruitment or contact is part of the run. This small synthetic pilot does not generalize to a reader population.

No reported omnibus quality win from single-investigator screening. For each arm, report generated count, provider input and completion tokens, and source errors. Inspect at least the complete notice and science outputs for admissions/conditions and observed/inferred claims. Do not infer outcome gains from CI success. Earlier work R9.29–R9.33 remains development history.
