## 3. G10 takes over a *different central research question*

The research object moves from correctness of narrowly bounded constraints, selective editing and inferential authority to **the prose generated for a Korean reader**.

**G10-P1 question:** Given matched sources, goals, audience and base LLM, does a lightweight discourse planning + context-dependent Korean realization architecture improve Korean paragraph/document quality, source fidelity or efficiency compared with strong direct generation, rich prompting and diverse-candidate baselines?

The implementation must generate full Korean passages, not merely judge a completed text or prove a provenance invariant. Its output is writing; its authority is actual comparative writing quality.
