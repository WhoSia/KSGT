# G9-P53 P0 — Genre-Conditioned Writing Authority Reconstitution

## Core discovery from actual 14B baseline

A generic instruction not to invent anything beyond five sparse facts can be **incompatible with the desired open-ended fictional prose task**. A scene writer often needs texture, incidental gestures, timing, weather or interiority. The scientific report, in contrast, cannot assert unmeasured facts as observations. Treating both as a single undifferentiated `NO_NEW_EVENTS` restriction confounds model competence with a defective task contract.

This is a **hypothesis about instrumentation exposed by the Qwen3-14B B-only readback**, not evidence that fictional additions were permitted in the previous preseal, nor evidence KSGT improved anything.

### Factorized source authority

`F`: protected source commitments — who/what/when/how much, negation and comparator direction, named actor/referent, explicit unknowns.
`L`: author-granted creative license — optional *fictional* atmosphere, sensory detail, innocuous motion and rhythm; never override `F`, never invent identity or strong causal/factual claims without permission.
`E`: epistemic modality — what was observed, hypothesized or unknown; science-specific inferences must be labelled hypotheses and cannot masquerade as measured variables.
`W`: writer goal — desired genre, voice, tone, perspective, degree of ambiguity, and desired quantity of additional texture.

**Scientific mode:** `L=MINIMAL`, and only externally defensible, clearly marked hypotheses may be considered separately from observations; `F` and `E` take priority.

**Narrative mode:** `L=AUTHOR_DEFINED`, where fictional detail may enrich scene texture without violating protected facts. A generated movement, wind sound or unspoken intention is not automatically a source contradiction, but it must be audited against the explicit permission `W`. Hidden character identity, causal or factual certainty, especially prohibited additions, remain blocked.

**Writer-controlled references:** `그중` ambiguous group identity should not always be resolved to a repetitive explicit noun phrase. The author may license ambiguity as a stylistic effect. The v0.7 mechanism must be conditional on `W`.
