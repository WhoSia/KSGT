Counts: 8B 1/2 two-paragraph format, 14B 3/4, combined 4/6. This is a six-output exposed development audit, **not** a Korean model benchmark, population statistic or real human writing-quality measure.

**Theoretical consequence:** A Korean writing generator must jointly preserve (F) protected factual entailments, (E) observed/inferred/unknown epistemic status, (L) the writer-authorized scope of fictional invention, and (W) voice, goal and intentional ambiguity. Generic `no additions` instructions can overconstrain fiction; unrestricted additions can falsify science. An evidence-witnessed `그중` rewrite is a *candidate*, and KEEP remains a legitimate output. F/E/L/W flags are not an automatic semantic judge.
