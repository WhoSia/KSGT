## Decision: the obvious large archive is an insufficient target by itself

Do not indiscriminately mine *every* human Wikipedia edit as a label for literary quality. Adding a template, updating metadata, correcting a date or reversing vandalism constitutes valuable social collaboration but is not a demonstration that the edit improved lexical choice, sentence flow or aesthetic prose. Equating chronological after with better text would train **formatting selection** and make KSGT drift even further from the goal set in 2026-07-15 KSGT 1.

The initial two retrieved source pairs do not furnish independent human preference labels and cannot be counted as positive prose revisions. This does NOT mean the G8 source program lacked value; the raw lineage and rights management remain useful for supervised sample selection, and the same files might yield syntactic examples after actual alignment.

## Better next-source experiment

Find a **small rights-verified genuinely edited prose cohort** with natural paragraph before/after text, not metadata or autogenerated text. Group by original author/document and chronological source parent; classify actual edit operation classes by character spans and linguistic role:
- lexical collocation/noun–verb pairing,
- sentence/paragraph information order,
- omission and clause condensation,
- referential realization and dropped subjects,
- epistemic scope preservation,
- genre-specific sentence-final rhythm / narrative tension.

Before modeling, adjudicate whether the changed span is a stylistic edit, fact addition, error correction or mixed; human-after chronology is not an author quality reward. Use known authentic revision traces, not synthetic self-edits, to construct *candidate proposal preferences* for a compact learned Korean realizer or retrieval-conditioned rewrite model. Licensing and source contamination must be handled before any model training.

**Methodological stop:** do not spend another R5 cycle merely building HTML, auditable surveys, an arbitrary regex detector or a perfect data ledger. The next scientific deliverable should be **natural complete Korean writing generated/edited using actual author revision patterns** against a strong direct model and realistic costs. Source audits should be proportional to that deliverable, not displace it.
