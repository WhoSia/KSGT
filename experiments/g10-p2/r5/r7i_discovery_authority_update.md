# What R7i actually changes
A draft can discover a relation, but it can also create a false premise. A plan can prevent omission, but can also crystallize an invalid measurement interpretation. The next theoretical object is not a binary plan/draft switch; it is the provenance and reversibility of intermediate relations. Represent candidate discourse relation r with support(r), status(r), and dependency(r) on immutable source facts. Changing an explanatory relation may be legal even if the resulting wording differs. Replacing a required event or promoting a generated claim to source truth is not. Distinguish new assertion, derived assertion, rhetorical ordering and intentionally licensed fiction. Intermediate text is evidence of model behavior, not authority. The FILTER and RETURN cases demonstrate that simply restating source authority in the final prompt does not prevent persistence. TILE demonstrates that a valid detail can disappear between drafts; control should track obligations rather than reify all intermediate sentences. The next experiment should test source-grounded revision of relations with fresh task and direct single-call rival; avoid mandatory multi-call writing and artificial human packet.
