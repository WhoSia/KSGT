## Interpretive limitations and counterexamples
MEMORY neutral and cue+limit: current lack of memory was treated as contradiction of prior knowledge, a temporal/epistemic scope error. The cue response said prior knowledge was not explicitly given, ignoring a plausible lexical prior-knowledge implication of forget. PACKAGE cue: exact count moved was treated as negation of a greater expected count; count realized and count expected are distinct variables. RESULT: model supported waiting for blue despite source only specifying timing and color, and even asserted that the color changed at five minutes, which is not identical to recording after five minutes. RETURN supports same-chair prior sitting with again; explicit uncertainty overrides its output, but the passage may be pragmatically infelicitous rather than successfully canceling a presupposition. EXHIBIT ignores scalar/additive signal of majo, ACCESS ignores still expectation; targets are narrower than general cue meanings and not proven linguistically obligatory. The six families differ in construction and meaning. Do not call all cues a single implicature class, do not declare semantic equivalence, and do not blacklist them. All ratings are prompted model interpretations, not independent gold or human acceptance. Control is easy explicit text, so 18/18 does not validate the target instrument.
