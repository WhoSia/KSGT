## Decision
Mixed/negative for promoting coupled layout as a reliable quality/fidelity advantage. Retain optional grouping for readable authoring, not mandatory. Main new practical issue: source facts can contain contextual knowledge or instructions about what not to ask; a writing model may recite a control instruction as if it were message content. A fact partition and paragraph-effect schedule does not distinguish intended payload from contextual/control information. Next isolate role authority and preserve actual natural writing with a one-call intervention, rather than more arbitrary planning labels. Distinguish internal context from communication obligations, literal content from control constraints, and negative facts that genuinely need expression from prohibitions on the writer. Test with strong same-information baseline, measure exact text and human-readable differences, keep no-promote gate. Literary rhythm/lexical texture remains a mainline target. No general semantics certification or independent human evidence.
