# R7i — Writing as discovery
Galbraith (2009), Writing as discovery, BJEP Monograph Series II 6, pp.5–26. Institutional record https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/337490/ (403 on open); publicly indexed full-text transcription https://www.academia.edu/7168566/Writing_as_discovery, sections on synthesizing ideas, connectionist model and conclusion inspected. Explicit knowledge retrieval/manipulation and implicit knowledge constitution are complementary, not a universal rough-draft advantage. The synthesis is conditioned on implicit constraints and earlier utterance; it is not evidence that a transformer has human dispositions. Transfer: fixed source facts and changeable rhetorical relations must have distinct authority.
