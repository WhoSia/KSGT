## Decision
Do not replace LLM with handwritten finite grammar. A candidate mechanism earns priority only if it improves a full draft or reduces demonstrated revision burden with source/task preserved. Use natural complete Korean draft, genre-sensitive constraints, legitimate contrast positive control, optional KEEP; compare same model and information under same one-call max token budget. Test exact-surface transactional edits without claiming semantic verification. Cost/quality and independent evidence remain separate. No new human recruitment/training, no NIKL transfer. Architecture remains open; no forced new generation number.
