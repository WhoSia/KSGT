# R6l — Heterogeneous discourse, preregistered developer comparison
2026-11-27 · development only · QUALITY_HOLD

Six newly authored source works (archive narrative, science report, facility notice, team message, list narrative, exhibition report), six information units each, two paragraphs. Compiled candidate texts, local actor focus, known nominal referents, required predecessor relations and extra-fact flags were authored before live generation. Each direct arm receives all six natural-language facts and same genre/paragraph constraints, not the compiled answer or candidate pool. One GPT-OSS 120B Groq call per work at temperature zero, max 1000 tokens, no retries, stops on incomplete response. Compiled arm uses no provider calls but has substantial manual engineering costs. These are not source-work-independent evaluation or blinded human ratings. The exact order+variant optimizer uses length among admissible candidates; the same-pool length-only control drops contextual constraints but still excludes explicitly tagged extra facts. Exhaustive mode removes memoization and checks the same recurrence; it is not an independently implemented oracle. Precommit evaluation: preserve all six facts, source attribution, actor/object identity, chronology and causal scope, uncertainty and proposal vs completion, two-paragraph genre; review complete outputs, keep ambiguous readings separate. No new NIKL access or training. Do not infer automatic language understanding from supplied tags. In particular, globally unique nominal references are conservative and cannot substitute for pragmatic salience or referent inference.
