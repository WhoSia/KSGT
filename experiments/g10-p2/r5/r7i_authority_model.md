# R7i — source authority versus emergent rhetorical relationships
Define immutable task/source F, permissible creative expansion L, provisional discourse graph G_t, realized text U_t and revision operations Δ_t. A proposed relation can have status ENTAILED (under declared inference model), HYPOTHESIS (must be qualified), LICENSED_CREATIVE (only within task), or UNSUPPORTED. Generation may change G_t (including replacing its rhetorical goal ordering), not mutate F silently. In closed report/notice, unprovided facts cannot become true by being generated. In fiction permitted invented details may be proposed, but they cannot contradict fixed events or silently override the requested intent. A later draft is not a new world-fact authority. Positive operation: put the practical question first, then explain the relevant tool. Counterexample: invent a reason for a narrator's action because a draft mentioned it. R7h residual interpretation is distinct from invention. No claim of automatic inference correctness.
