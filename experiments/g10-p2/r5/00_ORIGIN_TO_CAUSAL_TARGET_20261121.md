# G10-P2 R5 · Origin-to-mechanism decision record (2026-11-21)

Status: RESEARCH QUESTION / NO NEW PERFORMANCE CLAIM.
Authority: the user's KSGT 1–25 exported chat prompts on Google Drive, G10-P1 closure, G10-P2 R1–R4b native evidence. Historical interpretations below are not independent experimental findings.

## Recovered scientific North Star
The first KSGT prompt on 2026-07-15 asked for a Korean writing tool correcting gratuitous 'A가 아니라 B' contrasts, imported English clauses and predictable AI boilerplate. Subsequent prompts set deliberately ambitious targets across scientific, critical, conversational and literary genres; asked for justified theoretical coordinates rather than decorative vectors; suggested attention-like or lightweight architectures; and insisted on usable generated prose, not a detector alone. The user repeatedly wanted a small and effective system, even if it reuses pretrained models.

The archive includes direct reversals of assistant judgments: in KSGT 24, the reader preferred a more readable Korean candidate over an ostensibly stronger mechanically reasoned candidate. KSGT 15–16 show unacceptable model output, and KSGT 25 warns that well-engineered evaluator/source-governor machinery has drifted from *writing*. Historical self-simulated critic opinions must not be described as independent human preferences.

Operational contract: **evaluate the reader-facing complete text; assess licensed meaning separately; ask whether any KSGT state mechanism adds value beyond the same powerful base model and same information in plain text; count all marginal cost.**

## Realized-passage object, not proxy-task success
For each independent source work w, compare completed texts using (1) reader task usability, (2) reader-reported first edit burden, (3) independent source fidelity, (4) overall writing preference and genre suitability, and (5) complete call/token/latency/annotation costs. A syntactic guard, self-audit, self-generated questionnaire or 'same key phrase removed' is no substitute for any of these five.

Preserve the expressive objective: appropriate Korean repetition, ellipsis, relation, cadence and literary texture can be licensed; a blanket ban on AI-looking tokens would erase legitimate style. A human style is not 'randomly irregular text' and is not an AI-detector avoidance metric.

## Historical negative evidence that constrains R5
- G10-P1: no independently demonstrated superiority; preserve all null and mixed outcomes.
- G10-P2 R2: 40/40 author-created assertion agreement in both two-way and finer five-way prompts; additional classification costs do not imply prose gain.
- G10-P2 R2b: on older actual prose, assertion label agreement with author decisions was 20/22 for binary and 16/22 for richer extraction; role/scope failure survived.
- G10-P2 R4a: 12 source works/24 prose candidates, 60 actual calls; source self-audit missed invented promises and locations; 49,978 total experiment tokens versus 6,963 direct-writer-only tokens; human judgments 0.
- G10-P2 R4b: 20 actual model requests, 3/6 initial targeted spans removed by strict PATCH vs 6/6 by full rewrite; 4/4 NO_TARGET unchanged in each arm. Both arms had *zero independent semantic or reader gold*. Removing a span may substitute a different unsupported assertion.
- An exact-print case with developer-provided allowed sentences is a mechanical contract, never free-form Korean semantic comprehension.

## Decision against 'complexity laundering'
Novelty and usefulness are separate: provenance contracts, finite-state guards and grammatical libraries might be engineering contributions, but their complexity cannot be cited as Korean writing-quality evidence. If (state as text) and (executable state controller) are not distinguishable in a source-work-disjoint, compute-matched, independently reviewed whole-prose test, freeze architectural growth and simplify.

The next contribution is an intervention whose necessity can be defeated, not another large theoretical title. Claims about learned small models, surpassing Transformers, high literary quality, or independent reader preference remain explicitly UNESTABLISHED.
