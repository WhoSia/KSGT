## Decision
Preserve role annotations as an authoring contract and optional prompt compression, not a mandatory intervention. Mixed/negative for reliably superior fidelity. Next return to expressive quality, with source-constrained versus fiction-permitted expressive latitude and rhythm; avoid repeated tiny prompt layout studies. A longer coherent passage with an independently defined human-facing task is more useful than more role enums. Keep explicit source preservation and strong direct baseline. No external literature added. No native port: local record serialization is not the observed bottleneck, fast-anything and rustc unavailable.
