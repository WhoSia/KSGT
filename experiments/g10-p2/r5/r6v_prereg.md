# R6v — Task-conditioned cadence revision on complete prose
2026-11-27. Prospective development trial; no independent human gold.

R6u and R6j show that role marking and fixed local realizers do not ensure expressive writing. Instead, test a deterministic draft-dependent observation module on four new multi-paragraph pieces: closed-world reflective essay, creative-license station fiction, closed-world literary refrain, and parallel science explanation. Each receives the same facts, license, task, strong genre-aware system instruction, and model (GPT-OSS120B, temp 0, max2400). Three outputs per work: DIRECT first draft, REVISE same draft plus strong revision instruction, CADENCE same draft and identical revision instruction plus diagnostic observations derived from that draft. Direct is shared; 12 actual calls, no retries; order of revision arms alternated. Both revision routes two calls including direct. Input cost not identical. No private material sent.

Diagnostics are exact first whitespace-token runs of three or more, or three consecutive sentences of similar nonspace length (range/mean <=0.2, minimum length >=12, not science genre). Skip paragraphs with quotes or unmatched terminal punctuation; split only punctuation followed by whitespace, not a full Korean parser. Exclude declared intentional refrain or protected science parallel opening from a window. Select at most one cue per paragraph and three per work, prefer lexical repetition. If no cue, CADENCE prompt is identical to REVISE; count separately, never fabricate a cue. These are arbitrary development thresholds for candidate localization, not human naturalness metrics. Review flags and full drafts, including preserving intentional refrain, creative latitude versus unsupported event claims, and final event/known facts. Report costs, outputs, failure cases. No claim of statistical power, independent human preference or universal stylistic value. Do not promote mandatory diagnostic revision absent evidence. Keep original drafts and exact dynamic parent receipts.
