## Research decision
Three independent objectives: source authorization (asserted, presupposed, inferred), discourse coherence / intended referent recovery, and recipient utility/quality. A plausible inference is not automatically a source violation; distinguish intended defeasible interpretation, genre license and unintended new commitments. Plan adherence and human preference remain separate. Use actual existing texts for a bounded reader-packet review rather than generate more lightly differentiated prompts. Independent humans still required for confirmatory preference. No new model call or CI claim. Read-held two PDFs substantive sections, three abstracts; no full acquisition. Literature grounded prioritization, not empirical performance evidence.
