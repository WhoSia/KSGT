## Complete-text investigator review (unblinded)
- Report: DIRECT removes the unlicensed contrast but still adds vague experimental purpose, turns not-recorded into not-confirmed and adds a recommendation. PATCH preserves the false-contrast opening in modified words, weakens the source's explicit unknown cause to a recommendation to review, and also changes record scope. Both improve local verbosity; neither preserves all source nuance. Direct reads more compactly.
- Explain: neither follows requested conversational banmal. DIRECT uses labels/headings, changes register, conflates heat energy/internal energy in restated question. PATCH explicitly claims formal register is better in reasons, preserves unnecessary praise/contrast/conclusion, drops same comparison reference and strengthens possibility to categorical. Both correctly convey core mass distinction, but a local edit interface cannot recover writer–reader stance on its own.
- Notice: legitimate correction contrast maintained in both. Draft already acceptable; no KEEP in either. PATCH changes polite request to a declarative statement with unjustified claim that the original polite ending is inappropriate; DIRECT rephrases without material content gain. No preference certification.
- Literary: both eliminate unsupported abstraction; both remain largely short sentence sequences, no fresh permitted shape image. PATCH creates doubled whitespace at deletion boundary and replaces the pronoun with a repeated subject; DIRECT uses '바라보았다', slightly more varied. Both largely preserve scene. No general literary quality ranking.
- Essay: DIRECT collapses the required two paragraphs into one and retains an abstract concluding claim; PATCH preserves two paragraphs but intensifies the abstract conclusion and reintroduces formulaic contrast. One reason says the sentence was split when it was not. Neither fixes global essay progression satisfactorily.

## Decision
Negative for promoting mandatory exact patch format as a writing-quality or efficiency improvement. Mechanical validity is a useful optional editor tool, not quality authority; retain KEEP, local repair and untouched span preservation, but no default compulsory edit interface. The key failures are source-specific interpretation, audience/register, whole-document organization and stylistic effect, not patch mechanics. Provide post-hoc authored development targets in r6r_reference_drafts.json; these are not independent human gold or scored reference. The literary example remains a candidate, not a pragmatic proof. Next test prospectively matched task-level writer planning on new whole-draft genres with strong direct baseline and preservation check; consider natural in-context realization and longer literary material, not additional regex policing. Reuse R6n–q modules only when they address a concrete generation failure. No new external papers introduced; central missing list read but not modified. Maintain OPEN and independent human quality gate.
