## Theory
For a deterministic obligation map A=a(M), H(M|U,C)=H(A|U,C)+H(M|A,U,C). Wrong-obligation mass is separately 1-P(A=a*|U,C). Low entropy is not correctness; residual expressive interpretation need not be removed. The five finite witnesses and 360 exhaustive rational distributions test an identity and implementation, not a cognitive model. See r7h_obligation_partition.md, r7h_temporal_resolution.md and the two Korean semantic reading notes. Literary recontextualization is a separate hypothesis, not an entropy-maximization objective.
