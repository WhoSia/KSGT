# KSGT G10-P2 R5h — Korean Semantic-Role Binding Under Source-Preserving Surface Realization (PREREG, 2026-11-21)

**Frozen in Git before any R5h model inference.** This is a **new source-exposed development experiment** on **eight previously untested, researcher-written Korean literary micro-scenes**, not a general writer, a Korean parser, an independent aesthetic gold, a neural model or a claim of computational-linguistic novelty.

## What is being experimentally compared?

The earlier 16-call R5g experiment found that a correct DAG topological order **still moved the folded paper from the character who held it to another character**. Korean omitted subjects, subject switches, noun particles and speaker/object ownership need to be handled **during actual surface construction**. A purely source-labeled prompt is insufficient to guarantee this, while post-hoc exact-span edits can break discourse seams.

Test the following **three REAL full-text routes** on eight truly new source scenes with **48 preauthored predicate clauses** and **two named characters per scene**:
1. **DIRECT120B**: model GPT-OSS 120B, temperature 0, max 1450. Source frame facts, character roles, license and the whole-scene goal are given. It freely composes a finished Korean micro-scene.
2. **ROLE_COMPILED120B**: same model and sampling parameters, **one model call**, but the response can contain only **finite lexical variant/subject-form selection IDs**. The actual Korean text is built **by executable Node code** from *fixed, role-preserving source predicates* and subject particles; all six frames are emitted, not just the selected parts. The selector chooses `variant` 0/1 and `subjectForm` TOPIC/NOM/ZERO; the compiler rejects unlicensed ZERO when the subject is not the active center, has a competing newly mentioned actor or is itself the first mention. `speaker/actor/holder` identities come from the source schema and are never writable by the model. No invented events or independent factual sentences may pass into the compiled text through the selection channel.
3. **COMPILED_DEFAULT (0 model calls)**: exactly the same executor, selecting source-clause variant 0 and a conservative explicit Korean NP. It provides an ablation for whether an LLM selector adds anything to the static program.

**Total planned new provider calls: 16 (=8 DIRECT + 8 constrained selectors).** Default deterministic text is 8 additional complete texts requiring **zero provider calls**. Historical G10-P2 starts at **324 real calls**, therefore successful R5h would yield **340**, with raw receipts tied to every prompt hash. The experiment is **NOT** 24 provider generations.

## Genuine executable intervention and falsifiable limits

Each of 48 source frames has `frameID, kind, subjectEntityID|null, semanticSubject (if present), original fact and exactly two distinct author-written subjectless Korean predicate-clause variants`. A scene also lists two named characters and 1–2 immutable properties/negative licenses. All clause alternatives are **researcher-written and vetted as equivalent before any model inference**; this supervision is expensive and makes the method **non-generalizing** at this stage. Neither a fluent, free-form neural realization model nor automatic semantic role extraction is implemented.

For subject omission, surface realization must consider whether the immediately prior **animate frame** had the same agent; a background frame does not itself authorize dropping an actor name after an intervening subject switch. Code tracks `lastAnimateSubject`, `seenEntities` and whether an entity is new; zero forms are allowed only after a **consecutive same-agent clause**. In other cases, explicit Korean subjects using the Jamo-derived subject-case particle and topic marker are rendered. No generic "그는/그녀는" replacements. This is intentionally conservative and *not a linguistic claim that all other Korean ellipsis is impossible*.

The model returns only JSON with six choices; invalid or unsafe choices are **not appended to generated prose**, and the per-frame rejection/fallback reason is written with the original raw model response. Any parse failure that leads to default is counted, not quietly disguised as a successful selection. The compiler also emits an immutable trace mapping each output clause to exact `frameID`, subject identity, selected predicate and source parent; no uncontrolled actor-changing generation is admitted inside that code path. It cannot guarantee that an author-written predicate variant is semantically correct, or that a **human reader finds the assembled text beautiful**.

**Crucial:** The compiled route will likely have lower literary variety and less natural cadence than free 120B. If so, document this negative trade-off; do not install a mandatory semantic compiler, add huge HTML reader forms or call a fixed-source template toy a novel language model. If lexical/grammatical benefits appear, a next phase would require automatic source-framing and low-cost natural-language expression proposals under genuinely source-dependent constraints and a cost-matched strong direct baseline.

## Unseen author-synthetic eight scene conditions (frozen before new calls)
Eight separate stories in a record room, night library, art museum, rehearsal room, greenhouse walkway, seaside observatory, old bookshop, and museum workshop. Each contains two named characters, exactly six factual frames, at least one material possession/action binding, and a forbidden future action/dialogue or unrevealed information; different source task IDs than R5g. The scenes are synthetic **researcher-designed controls** and do not establish natural human-authored literary corpora.

Model calls alternated so half the cases execute DIRECT first and half ROLE_COMPILED selector first. Direct and compiled inputs use same source frame identities/facts; compiled additionally sees the finite executable candidate pool, so extra prompt tokens and access to hand-authored alternatives are **intervention resources**, not an isolated "same-token" contrast. No quality equivalence or direct superiority should be claimed from schema-only output conformity.

## Evaluation priorities
- Inspect **the actual completed source text, 120B direct prose, default compiled prose and selected compiled prose** together; flag who physically possesses a document/object or speaks and whether scene no-action limits are violated. The compiler's exact trace ensures its **own frame-level source identity** under trusted source fixtures, **not external semantic entailment**.
- For direct free text, a strong writer may realize a beautifully natural Korean scene and omit some explicit facts to preserve pacing; count source fact omissions separately from invented events; a free text with omission is not automatically worse than a clumsy six-clause list in a literary scene.
- Quantify exact provider prompt+completion tokens, additional source-alternative preparation effort, accepted vs rejected subject ellipsis decisions, duplicate formulations and visibly broken literary cadence. Distinguish automatic source-compliance counters from expert/aesthetic evaluation.
- The compulsory comparator is strong GPT-OSS 120B; no prompt-only assistant-vs-assistant "reward" or self-judgment is accepted.

## Verified prior art, not an invention claim
- Roh & Lee (2003), *An Empirical Study for Generating Zero Pronoun in Korean based on Cost-based Centering Model*, https://aclanthology.org/U03-1.pdf : **already an algorithm for Korean zero pronoun generation**, tested on 87 genre-mixed texts; our conservative subject rementioning is a tiny implementation of a known problem, **not new**.
- Hwang (2023), *The influence of discourse continuity on referential form choice*, https://doi.org/10.1037/xlm0001166 : Korean user experiments indicate subject/action continuity affects zero subject realization.
- *Separating Licensing from Interpretation: A Reassessment of Korean Null Arguments* (2026), https://www.elanguageresearch.org/archive/view_article?pid=lr-62-2-207 : distinguish omission licensing from omitted argument resolution; official abstract only checked.
- R5g exact source and full original [R5g paper](21_R5G_16_LIVE_KOREAN_PARAGRAPHS_ARGUMENT_OWNERSHIP_FAILURE_20261121.md) and [Drive original source and 16 texts](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pKtaViq3wwtVawNdBVOZcx0mSTAidvaK/view) provide explicit failure anchor.

**Success means more than source constraints:** This prototype should be rejected as a general writer unless native-Korean complete prose quality can beat direct generation at acceptable cost with provenance and non-synthetic inputs. No reader samples or semantic gold will be fabricated.
