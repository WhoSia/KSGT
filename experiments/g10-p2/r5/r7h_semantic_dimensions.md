# Separate dimensions, not a binary lexical gate
R7h synthesis from Lee 2018 and Choi 2026. Maintain (1) source world support (true/false/unknown), (2) speaker commitment or reported agent belief, (3) prior discourse availability, (4) assumed addressee common ground, (5) intended goal/action/state relation, (6) obligations and open interpretation. A proposition can be true and new, believed but false, known by both but not mutually recognized, or deliberately unspecified. "Previously knew" is not equivalent to "now remembers"; "again" may relate distinct events via purpose; "do" alternative is not necessarily already mentioned. These distinctions invalidate any one-bit fact-state or word blacklist as a complete Korean realization model. They do not establish that extra labels in the prompt improve writing. Candidate choice needs actual source-context and full prose; ambiguous cases remain open.
