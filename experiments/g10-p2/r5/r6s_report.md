## Complete-text investigator reading (unblinded; no independent preference evidence)
- Lab: DIRECT explicitly presents purpose, then repeats the numeric differences and speculates about other uncontrolled factors. REVISE loses the explicit purpose and makes the initial observed 24 degrees sound like a set condition; repeats the difference and speculative conclusion. REALIZE concisely reports both results and the single-trial/airflow limitation without speculative ending, but the explicitly assigned purpose (fact 1, first paragraph focus) is not stated as a purpose. A structurally valid plan does not ensure the planned communicative function is realized.
- Message: all use banmal. DIRECT omits the speaker's uncertainty about whether the friend opened the file, adds a future discussion and unnecessary repeated request/thanks. REVISE still omits that uncertainty and additionally drops the unresolved room-use condition. REALIZE explicitly retains the file uncertainty, but changes previous announcement to meeting agreement ('만나기로 했었지'), changes room-use uncertainty into room selection uncertainty ('연습실은 아직 정해지지 않았고'), and contains the typo '올려놨아'. The time change is not frontloaded as strongly as it could be. All three use the original and changed times and location.
- Scene: DIRECT adds flowing rain and sound, light through darkness, reflections, simultaneity ('그 순간'), and a causal gaze relation ('그 빛을 따라'); also '눈금' assumes clock type. REVISE removes some elaboration but retains multiple invented physical details and the gaze motivation. Both omit the clock's entryway location. REALIZE retains that location and the raindrops as beaded rather than flowing; avoids the invented causal gaze and simultaneity but adds the reflection on droplets and has repetitive '시계', redundant '마지막으로 그녀는'. It reads as a factual sequence, not necessarily a stronger literary passage.
- Essay: DIRECT and REVISE add opening/turning pages, density/checkmarks, conjecture about circles and a drawer, and abstract interpretation beyond the source. REALIZE is shorter and closer in its final reflection, but still invents taking out the notebook, densely filled pages and checkmarks; conflates unknown circle meaning with items recorded because important; adds causal '그래서' and shifts completed re-storage to decision ('보관하기로 했다'). None can be certified as faithful or independently preferred. The constraint is especially strict: expressive latitude does not authorize new events or mental states.

## Decision and next intervention
R6s is development evidence that task-level planning can alter final prose, not a confirmed quality improvement or cost advantage. Keep the plan mechanism available; do not make two-call planning mandatory. An explicit focus ID is insufficient: the final paragraph can omit its function even with all IDs assigned, while the original direct draft may realize it correctly. Source facts in a plan are inventory obligations, not a rigid sentence recipe; natural repeated reference is not a defect. Next develop a compact effect-bearing paragraph plan (what the reader needs to understand, rather than simply IDs/labels), with source-supported relation and scope, and test it against a strong same-information direct prompt at matched call count. Explore one-call and selective escalation before additional multi-stage overhead. Need longer independent briefs, blinded independent Korean reader/editing evidence, task-specific style evaluation, and external generalization. No training, no independent human evaluation. No new literature; do not modify central missing TXT merely because a count changed.
