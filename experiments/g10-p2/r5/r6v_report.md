## Decision and next intervention
Do not promote these cadence heuristics as quality ranking or default extra call; actual intervention coverage 1/4. Preserve optional transparent localization and deliberate refrain exceptions. Investigate source-content budget versus target length, genre-conditioned permission to elaborate, and functional discourse/imagery rather than more numerical stylistic detectors. A compact accurate paragraph may be better than padding to an arbitrary length. Compare genuinely longer source briefs (many supported facts/events) and shorter/flexible target constraints at matched task; isolate length pressure and license (not claims from the current four cases). Post-hoc alternate drafts illustrate improvement targets, not human gold or benchmark scores. Prioritize actual complete writing and independent blinded human reader/editing evidence. No new literature; central missing list read not modified. Native port not justified by provider-call-dominated workload; fast-anything and rustc CLI not found.
