# Interpretive partition model
Let finite possible interpretations be M, required answer map a:M→A, allowed interpretive latitude L, and a target answer a*. Let P(m|prefix) be a hypothetical distribution. Then H(M|prefix)=H(A|prefix)+H(M|A,prefix) since A is deterministic. Zero H(A) means only that all positive support agrees, not agreement with source a*. Requirement risk is P(a(M)≠a*). A text can preserve uncertainty among acceptable interpretations while all obligations are satisfied. Conversely high uncertainty may indicate source violation or a deliberately unresolved literary question; do not assume which without an independently specified author intent. Do not force a numerical target on H(M|A). 
For practical tasks, explicit conditions may be necessary because relevant context is unavailable to recipient. For narrative, an ambiguous emotional interpretation can remain open while the speaker/action is definite. This model is a hypothesis about representation and permissible decisions, not a natural language parser, human quality evaluator or automatic empirical probability estimator. Compare with prior R6y/z finite obligation bits, R7a/c reader probes: here multiple interpretations can share the same obligation value; uncertainty is not simply a missed bit.
