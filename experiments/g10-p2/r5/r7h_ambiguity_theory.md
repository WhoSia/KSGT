# R7h — Contextual uncertainty and expressive latitude
2026-11-28. Piantadosi, Tily & Gibson (2012), Cognition 122:280–291, DOI 10.1016/j.cognition.2011.10.004, author-hosted PDF https://colala.berkeley.edu/papers/piantadosi2012communicative.pdf . Sections 2.1/2.2 and discussion reviewed; page 283 image checked. Context reduces expected conditional entropy if informative; the result assumes comparable speaker/listener coding and contextual information. Corpus analyses concern English/German/Dutch lexical/phonological reuse; not Korean literary quality. A particular surprising context need not lower entropy. A confident but mistaken listener can have zero entropy.
KSGT hypothesis: distinguish ambiguity in isolated expression from ambiguity after the relevant prefix, and from deliberate unresolved interpretation. Let M denote an interpretation, C context, A the intended obligation answer (possibly a function of M). H(A|C,U) may vanish while H(M|C,U)>0. Thus not every distinguishable interpretation warrants another clause. This is an exact finite information-theoretic identity in a specified model, not a measurement of actual Korean minds. Source-supported world state, required answer, expressive license and inferred meaning must remain separate.
Falsifiers: a wrong low-entropy answer; missing necessary context; arbitrary intended partition; required event left unclear by the end; repetitive explication that lowers ambiguity without new communicative value. No automatic selection by entropy, no human survey.
