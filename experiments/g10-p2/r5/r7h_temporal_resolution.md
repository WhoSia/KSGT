# A temporal hypothesis for expressive prose
An early sentence can be ambiguous in isolation while later prose resolves an obligation without resolving every interpretation. Let A be an obligation answer and M all interpretations. For a prefix U_t, expected H(A|C,U_1:t+1) <= H(A|C,U_1:t) under a fixed joint probability distribution with actual conditional expectation; along one observed continuation entropy may increase. No claim that actual readers monotonically reduce entropy. In literary tasks, retained uncertainty within A's fiber may support reinterpretation; in a notice, uncertainty about mandatory action at terminal is a failure. Target is author/task-dependent, not maximize or minimize entropy. Test with actual continuations: same prefix, same events, distinct intended closure. A control with no required resolution must not be forced to explain. Negative cases: wrong confidence, overexplicit motive, unsupported causality, abrupt twist. Avoid artificial reader panel.
