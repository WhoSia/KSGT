## Research boundary
The candidate scientific delta is joint allocation of required/optional propositions, expressive permissions, reader prerequisites and actual termination as distinct transition effects, then checking whether an adaptive policy improves complete native Korean writing at comparable cost. Generalizing the finite authored representation, inducing/adjudicating semantic labels independently, modeling pragmatic inference and obtaining human evidence remain open. The solver can be optional instrumentation; strong direct remains the primary rival. Avoid new overhead when the direct writer suffices.
