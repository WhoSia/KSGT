**Prepared, not yet administered.** Single research-involved Korean reader. No external independent raters and no substantive human meaning/preference outcomes.

Both original authored briefs EX09 and EX10 belong to the historically exposed frozen P53 source batch; EX10 is a separate source work but not new external or prospectively unseen material. **U2 non-entailment:** The EX09 source documents same-size boxes, the same seed kind and water quantity, but says the illumination and indoor temperature were not separately measured and makes no statement of absolute background-condition equality. Two source-consistent countermodels with (i) equal and (ii) unequal background soil/light/temperature establish that equality is not entailed. This does not prove actual inequality or settle the Korean modal `동일한 초기 조건에서 성장하기를 기대할 수 있다`. Wording remains a human interpretation HOLD.

**EX10 source-admissibility challenge:** Original prose invents possible narrator experience (“time stopped”), a momentary pause, and omits the street crossing. Those potential issues are analyst hypotheses. The brief's facts and three unconfirmed dimensions are fixed; the newer candidate edits attempt different repairs, but no candidate receives independent semantic PASS before the source-based review.

One reader, policy-masked pseudorandom frozen candidate order and **two separately filled private instruments**:

1. **Part 1: naturalness, comprehension, flow (each 1–7), ties allowed;** record whether the text or policy hypotheses were previously seen. Reader records choices without source brief, without opening the decoder. Preserve the first ratings before proceeding.
2. **Part 2: original authored brief opened;** each of five source facts and three uncertainties inspected for SUPPORTED / PARTIAL / OMITTED / OVERCLAIM / CONTRADICTION / UNDETERMINED, with candidate phrase and explanation. Focus separately on EX09's weak-modal initial-condition inference and EX10's unstated emotional and behavioral content.
3. Only after both ratings and qualitative notes have been sealed should the decoding key be used to link ratings to policy. Repeating the evaluation after a delay would be within-person reliability only, not a second independent reviewer.

The participant's prior involvement and recognizable source-number labels mean **policy-name masking is not fully blind**. Same source, task instruction and display order design constrain variation, but policy-specific text length (EX10 121–206 codepoints) remains a confound. Do not treat 8 candidate paragraphs as 8 independent reader observations; do not claim population significance.
