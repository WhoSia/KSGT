## 'Humanize' research — WHAT is really supported
This inquiry targets **legitimate Korean reading/writing naturalness**, not AI-detector evasion, false personable identity or random irregularity.
1. [Park et al. (2024) Korean essays vs GPT-4](https://doi.org/10.31889/kll.2024.3.206.5) reports broader human syntactic distribution and differing NP/modifier vs VP/AP tendencies. Corpus signature **does not establish a causal rewrite operator**.
2. [2026 comparative Korean subject honorification](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acorp.2025.100171) reports both subject+predicate honorific alignment **99.93% AI / 71.99% ordinary conversation** in matched 1,453-instance comparisons and context-dependent L1 judgments. **Do not insert erroneous mismatches to imitate observed frequency**. Context/social relation and speaker stance explain choice.
3. [Shin, Kwon & Mun (2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-026-08299-1) cautions that LM surprisal does not capture gradual Korean social/politeness/processing effects; no single proxy = native naturalness.
4. [Zhao (2024) Korean academic introduction](https://doi.org/10.22832/txtlng.2024.56..005) and [Yang et al. 2024 English theme progression](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.system.2024.103494) warn against formulaic discourse moves and transferring another language's theme architecture untested.
5. [Cheng, Yu & Jurafsky 2025 HumT DumT](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1261/) separates **human-like interpersonal tone** from task usefulness and writer/reader quality. Anthropomorphism is not the target.
6. **New directly relevant syntax find:** [Shin (2017) *Partitive descriptions in Korean*, Glossa, DOI 10.5334/gjgl.143](https://doi.org/10.5334/gjgl.143) analyzes **partitives vs pseudo-partitive/quantitative** postnominal quantifier structures and kind-denoting DP. Thus P55's discrete count strict subset is a **bounded licensed special case**, **not** universal Korean partitive syntax.
