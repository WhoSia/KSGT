## P58 one-question engineering result
**Question:** Must the P55 strict integer/measure `그중` surface rule apply to all Korean partitive-looking constructions?

**Bounded negative answer with explicit semantic branch contract.** [`semantic_gate.cjs`](https://github.com/WhoSia/KSGT/blob/main/experiments/g9-p58/semantic_type_court/semantic_gate.cjs) requires caller-*declared* source semantics (referential discrete individuals, referential *set of kinds*, generic KIND_DOMAIN, mass/measure, event-part, unknown); declared discourse-given and local scope; exact unambiguous source quote; selection mode; and declared writer preference. Its behavior:
1. A discourse-given **referential** set with proper count-based membership may yield a **review-only** explicit noun phrase alternative, conditional on declared CLARIFY. KEEP is always first and no automatic preference is inferred. Referents are not independently validated.
2. A KIND_DOMAIN quantity may be pseudo-partitive/quantitative rather than anaphoric reference. A *discourse-given referential set of kinds* is a distinct case; do not reject it because it denotes kinds.
3. A mass/volume `half` or event partition is not an integer member-subset problem. **A fraction of three apples may be a coherent physical-material reading** and must not be declared ungrammatical merely because P55's strict discrete-unit equality failed. “Whole-set” quantity is not automatically syntactically wrong; it simply falls outside this deliberately proper-subset constructor.
4. Discourse new/unknown source, competing scopes, unproved quotation, CONTEXTUAL/KEEP writer objectives, and multiple `그중` reference spans preserve original or HOLD. Style/naturalness is neither inferred from genre label nor fixed by redundant noun repetition.
