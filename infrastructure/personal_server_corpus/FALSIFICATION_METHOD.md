Intervals use 500 deterministic ordinary source-component bootstrap draws, including zero-target components. Repeated targets and release aliases remain in the same uncertainty cluster. Intervals are conditional exploratory cohort uncertainty; they are not population confidence, independent confirmation or multiplicity-adjusted discovery claims. The orthographic and pair-coarsened views re-detect surfaces and change units and eligibility. They are declared measurement sensitivities, not sentence gold or equivalent measurements. It reports common-support coverage and uncertainty rather than extrapolating unsupported cells.
