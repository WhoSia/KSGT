They contained 151,391 structures, 1,368,770 paragraphs, 1,370 target-bearing structures and 1,424 literal partitive targets; 150,021 structures had no target. The bounded first-member parse independently completed 11,882 structures and 169 targets before the full inventory. Container and year are completely confounded: there is one selected member per year.

The alias pass verified 2,523 original occurrence witnesses across ten members as exact structures. An independent passage audit completed 3,673 earlier-source shards, 2,373,431 original structures and 24,964,658 original paragraphs, yielding 204 previously exposed linked candidate structures. It also found 43 candidate-to-candidate shared-long-passage links and 286 prior-exposure shared-long-passage links, plus one exact-structure prior exposure. No title-only relation was used. The 80-codepoint passage rule and indexed occurrence set do not prove global intellectual-work independence: unobserved revised text and unrecovered earlier excerpts remain possible. The entire 2025 source environment was excluded to cover a known incomplete early excerpt ledger.

The deterministic component gate assigned 795 records to machine development, 253 to frozen transfer, 115 to reserved late and 488 to quarantine. Dependency-component counts were 776, 243, 111 and 199 respectively. The frozen transfer contains 253 target-bearing structures, 261 targets, five containers and metadata years 2019–2023. Reserved-late inference was zero. The machine split is an operational separation, not a certification of semantic work identity.

## Falsification and claim ceiling

Pre-outcome authored tests found that D-v1 records a nominative-versus-accusative cue change yet makes the same proposal under a role swap. Moving an adverb before an otherwise similar numeric group creates a spurious two-word NP alternative and flips D to abstention. These formal counterexamples expose role-insensitive selection and segmentation/order sensitivity. They are synthetic software evidence, not natural-source semantic judgments. Coordination and quotation controls produced abstention as designed, while indirect speech, nested scope and genuine thematic relations remain outside the parser.

R4-R5's corrected prior counts remain R3 typed 101, R4 73, v3 82, descriptor 0, particle proxy 14, combined 0 and stage-separated 82 on 273 structures/281 targets. The original erroneous R3-zero V1 aggregation remains invalid and preserved. The present cohort is new, uses different source components and cannot be compared as a paired effect with R4-R5. Human-ground-truth fields, semantic accuracy and writing-quality effects remain NULL. P62 closure and P63 promotion are unsupported.
