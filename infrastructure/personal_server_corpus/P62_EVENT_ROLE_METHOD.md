# P62 fresh source-witnessed noun-phrase and event-cue court

Status: prospective machine-only method. The official stage remains G9-P60.

The sample frame is the first unused, previously unexposed annual JSON member for each observed original-document metadata year 2019–2023 in the existing source-opportunity list. R4-R3, R4-R4 and R4-R5 selected members are excluded before target counts or algorithm outcomes are read. Every selected raw member must match the completed-ingestion SHA-256, byte count and document count at end of original ZIP parsing. Target-positive structures are audited against prior source keys, all indexed original occurrences, exact contiguous-unit/stream relationships and shared passages of at least 80 codepoints. Unknown revisions and normalized-only cross-ID links are quarantined. Absence of a detected exact passage is not proof of global intellectual-work independence.

Verified text-dependency components receive a deterministic 70/20/10 machine-development, frozen-transfer and reserved-late split. No reserved-late structure is submitted to inference. The target unit is a literal predeclared Korean partitive expression within one original structure. Each target uses the same original-unit coordinate and a 1,024-codepoint prefix for the inherited graph. The outcome is an admitted source-fragment proposal, never semantic antecedent correctness.

The prospective arms on the identical admitted graph are: A, the unchanged R5 exact descriptor policy; B, the nearest eligible original source fragment with a 48-codepoint competition abstention; C, the unchanged R5 particle/group policy; D, a source-bound surface relation requiring a preceding visible noun-form candidate, immediately following particle cue, visible same-clause predicate-form candidate and no unresolved quotation or coordination reason. D records one-token/two-token noun-boundary and multiple same-clause predicate-form alternatives as exact original spans and abstains when more than one is visible. Its quotation stack covers curly double and single delimiters with mismatch HOLD; indirect speech and other quotation conventions remain unparsed. Frozen guarded v3 is retained as a reference mechanism. D does not infer thematic AGENT or PATIENT from nominative or accusative case. The required phrase and predicate strings are intentionally crude and may suppress real cases or accept false ones; their failures are measured rather than silently repaired in the frozen court.

The primary comparison is document-equal admitted proposal-rate difference, D minus each baseline, with paired document-clustered uncertainty. Component-clustered sensitivity is required because exact-source dependencies can cross documents; container and publication-year strata are descriptive with actual cluster counts. Zero-target source structures stay in the engineering census but are outside target-level proposal denominators. Negative controls include authored role swaps, constituent order, coordinated groups, quotation boundaries, future-source rejection and source-digest mismatch. Public authored examples test software invariants only. No human labels are read or created.

An event predicate adjacent to a source group and a case particle together form a *surface witness relation*. They do not establish that the noun phrase denotes an event participant, that the event is asserted in the narrator's world, or that a later partitive refers to the group. A simple proximity rule can outperform this conservative relation on proposal coverage while being less meaningful; neither outcome measures reference accuracy. A coordination witness does not reveal whether group members overlap. A quote-scope mismatch cannot be repaired by matching particle categories.

Before corpus outcomes, an original synthetic role-swap test shows that D-v1 records a changed nominative/accusative cue but leaves its proposal unchanged. D-v1 therefore cannot itself establish role-sensitive disambiguation. A separate constituent-order probe fails an invariance demand: moving the adverb "어제" before the source group creates an additional crude two-word NP-boundary candidate and turns a proposal into an abstention, even though the visible group and case marking remain. This segmentation-sensitive negative result is frozen rather than repaired after seeing corpus outcomes. A later architecture would need a separate development lane and fresh evaluation evidence.

The strongest falsifier of D as a useful coverage mechanism is high abstention or zero gain across independent source containers; of D as a reference theory, any naturally occurring non-coreferential group with all its mechanical requirements. Such a semantic counterexample cannot be certified without independent linguistic evidence, so the present court has a strict machine-only claim ceiling. Original licensed text and private identities remain on the server.
