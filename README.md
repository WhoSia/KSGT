# KSGT — Korean Stylistic Generation Theory

KSGT studies **how context, information structure, discourse, syntax, register, and factual commitments become natural Korean prose**. The research objective is a passage-level Korean-writing system that can be judged for naturalness and factual integrity against a generic LLM baseline—not AI detector evasion, not a string-blacklist, and not a general proposer/approval/governance theory.

**KRC v0.1** compiled typed meaning/discourse plans, executed 18 controlled B/A/K outputs, and revealed a severe monolithic Korean prose format failure (K 0/6 exact two-paragraph adherence). The non-neural source-fact control preserved 30/30 licensed atom strings, which is **not** proof of Korean writing naturalness.

**KRC v0.2** executed two new Korean writing passages through a generator G, separate witness critic V and deterministic composer. Its [bounded CI](https://github.com/WhoSia/KSGT/actions/runs/37727690693) passed: 10 local model clauses, exactly 2 paragraph-structured outputs, but **9/10 model clauses were literal source copies**, and there were no spontaneous repair cycles. Separate [semantic false-negative tests](https://github.com/WhoSia/KSGT/actions/runs/37728066629) showed the lexical critic missed negation, comparison and event-action reversals. A [precommitted repair stress court](https://github.com/WhoSia/KSGT/actions/runs/37728285311) exercised 3 real model repairs for 3 deliberately injected defects; all passed mechanical checks, but one contained a Korean orthographic error. These are **mechanical transport findings, not evidence of better Korean prose or true GAN training**.

**KRC v0.3 (2026-10-08): Decoding competence reconstitution.** The former KRC v0.1 human reader comparison was withdrawn as scientifically invalid because many B/A/K passages failed basic syntax, logic, source fidelity and the actual writing task. A fixed, SHA-256 verified **Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 Q4_K_M** model executed [6 fresh B(clean) / U(plain plan) / K(typed plan) passages](https://github.com/WhoSia/KSGT/actions/runs/37732682355). The [frozen output readback](https://github.com/WhoSia/KSGT/actions/runs/37733414010) shows better-looking paragraph form, but **source/content errors in all three arms**: baseline science speculated incorrectly about controlled water quantities; U invented `오후 4시까지` on a sign; K leaked `자료 1에 따르면` and inserted unsupported scene affect. The basic fact/genre **competence gate still fails**, despite 4B. This is not a KRC superiority result. Canonical verdict: `artifacts/g9_p53_krc_v03_decoder_gate_verdict.json`, archived under [Drive KRC v0.3](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1oV9xfafzRmexTzAL1Px9pkIjE2HM_yxv).

**KRC v0.4 (2026-10-08): First executable Korean construction microgrammar.** Unlike v0.1–v0.3 prompt-fed LLM planning, v0.4 constructs two new finite source briefs (10 typed Korean fact frames) and can produce [four distinct two-paragraph Korean passages](https://github.com/WhoSia/KSGT/actions/runs/37742686521) without any LLM call. Five clauses undergo strictly source-licensed constituent reordering. The code also enforces Hangul coda-aware case particles, given-context topic constraints, one evidence-limited `다만` connective, and hard failures for predicate-polarity, comparison and causal violations. **11/11 CI tests passed**. This is a narrow source-attested grammar: unchanged internal semantic records and conserved constituents do not guarantee general Korean naturalness or pragmatically equivalent emphasis. The real research bridge ahead is context-licensed Korean clause linking, ellipsis and genuinely improved full prose. [Formal evidence receipt](https://github.com/WhoSia/KSGT/blob/main/artifacts/g9_p53_krc_v04_terminal_readback.json), [Drive original outputs](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1R4f-_U4vFwR03XyX_LiT38OvNwqUn3Oa).

**KRC v0.5 (2026-10-08): Actual clause composition and recoverable subject omission.** A new source-attested finite Korean grammar produces [four complete two-paragraph texts](https://github.com/WhoSia/KSGT/actions/runs/37745597095) from two fresh five-event contexts; two `-고` event joins and two recoverable actor omissions reduce each composed passage from five sentences to four. **8/8** tests pass including actor, polarity, comparison, causal-warrant, antecedent and paragraph-boundary counterexamples. These are **manually licensed constructions** with source event provenance, not a theorem of Korean meaning equivalence or improved naturalness. The unresolved `그중 한 컵` antecedent and productive verb morphology are the next actual construction questions. [Canonical v0.5 readback](https://github.com/WhoSia/KSGT/blob/main/artifacts/g9_p53_krc_v05_terminal_readback.json), [Drive archive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1K7BX-p0S3AXP6yUwK12Jt3L1lmrkVEBC).

### Research return / anti-drift

The next substantive target is a **Korean construction calculus**: source-grounded predicate–argument roles, polarity/negation, comparison direction, warranted discourse relations, context-licensed topic/focus and ellipsis, endings/register, rhythm and lexical texture. A valid surface operation must have a Korean-specific linguistic point and refutable source-grounded obligations. Do not automatically add a PIA/governor/consensus/approval stage or optimize a scalar “humanlike” discriminator. Do not confuse literal source copying with style improvement.

**KRC v0.1 blind survey RETIRED** — users inspected gross task, causal, factual and Korean-language failures in both arms. Do not recruit readers or interpret this sample as contextual naturalness. Its historical files are kept only for failure audit; see `artifacts/g9_p53_krc_v01_invalidity_ruling.json`. The current prospective competence gate `experiments/g9-p53/krc_v03/constitution.json` first tests ordinary coherent Korean writing with the fixed 4B Qwen3-Instruct decoder on fresh source briefs; if B is inadequate, do not score K against it. Human comparative evaluation is not authorized until a meaningful baseline exists.

The repeatedly used 24-item packet is **not** a fresh confirmatory evaluation pool.
